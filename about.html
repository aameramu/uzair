About U & I Consultancy

Trusted accounting and tax professionals serving businesses with practical financial guidance.

Our Story

U & I Corporate Financial & Tax Consultancy provides corporate financial consultancy, tax consultancy, registration, audit support, bookkeeping, and strategic planning services for businesses and individuals.

Our mission is to empower businesses with accurate financial information, professional guidance, and useful insight. We believe every company deserves dependable accounting support and a clear view of its financial position.

We support startups, SMEs, and growing companies with services built around compliance, transparency, and sustainable growth.
